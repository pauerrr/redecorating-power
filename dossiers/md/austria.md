# AUSTRIA — Documented Political Corruption Dossier

*Prepared for Domestic Data Streamers workshop. Compiled July 2026. Legal status verified against 2024–2026 sources. Strict rule applied throughout: only FINAL criminal convictions (confirmed by the OGH — Oberster Gerichtshof — or the appeal court OLG, or where the appeal deadline lapsed) or documented resignations are treated as established fact. Accused, under-investigation, or acquitted persons are named by role only, never asserted as guilty. Austria has seen several first-instance convictions overturned on appeal — this dossier tracks those reversals precisely.*

---

## 1. Country Snapshot

- **Transparency International CPI 2024**: Austria scored **67/100**, ranked **25th** — its worst result since the index's inception, down from 20th the prior year and 13th three years earlier. Sources: [Transparency.org](https://www.transparency.org/en/cpi/2024/index/aut), [ORF](https://orf.at/stories/3384464/)
- **CPI 2025** (published 10 Feb 2026): Austria improved to **69/100, rank 21st of 182**, tied with Belgium and the UAE — described as a "cautious trend reversal." Sources: [derStandard](https://www.derstandard.at/story/3000000307778/korruptionsranking-2025-sieht-leichte-verbesserung-fuer-oesterreich), [Transparency.org CPI 2025](https://www.transparency.org/en/cpi/2025)
- **Eurobarometer** (2025 survey): EU-wide, 69% of Europeans believe corruption is widespread in their country; 73% agree there is corruption in national public institutions. Austria sits well below high-perception states such as Greece (97%), Croatia (92%), and Portugal (91%), and above low-perception states like Finland (21%) and Denmark (28%). Sources: [Eurobarometer](https://europa.eu/eurobarometer/surveys/detail/3361), [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/my-europe/2025/07/23/do-eu-citizens-believe-that-their-local-and-national-public-institutions-are-corrupt)
- **Cost of corruption** (Prof. Friedrich Schneider, Johannes Kepler University Linz): up to **€15 billion** in economic damage to Austria in 2021, part of a rising trajectory: €12.0bn (2016) → €12.8bn (2017) → €11.9bn (2018) → €13.5bn (2019) → €14.4bn (2020). For comparison, the same methodology estimated Germany's 2021 cost at €234.1bn and the full EU-27's at €1,011.5bn. Sources: [derStandard](https://www.derstandard.at/story/2000141816140/korruption-duerfte-oesterreich-2021-bis-zu-15-milliarden-euro-gekostet), [FONDS professionell](https://www.fondsprofessionell.at/news/recht/headline/korruption-oesterreich-verliert-15-milliarden-euro-220827/)
- **GRECO — notably poor compliance**: In its Fourth Evaluation Round (MPs, judges, prosecutors), Austria satisfactorily implemented only **2 of 19 recommendations (~10.5%)** from 2017 — GRECO called compliance "globally unsatisfactory." A further interim report found only 3 of 19 recommendations satisfactorily addressed, 11 partly implemented, and 5 not implemented at all; Austria must submit further progress information by **31 May 2026**. The Fifth Evaluation Round (central government, law enforcement) found no system for strategically analyzing corruption risks facing top officials and weak "revolving door" standards. Sources: [Council of Europe](https://www.coe.int/en/web/portal/-/austria-compliance-with-anti-corruption-recommendations-for-mps-judges-and-prosecutors-is-globally-unsatisfactory-), [Council of Europe — Fifth Round](https://www.coe.int/en/web/greco/-/austria-must-improve-its-strategy-to-prevent-risks-of-corruption-in-government-and-in-law-enforcement)
- **Institutional note**: Austria abolished its 100-year-old constitutional "Amtsgeheimnis" (official secrecy provision) effective **1 September 2025**, replacing it with a Freedom of Information Act (Informationsfreiheitsgesetz). Critics note the absence of a mandatory evaluation clause and coverage gaps for journalists in its SLAPP-suit protections. Sources: [Forum Informationsfreiheit](https://www.informationsfreiheit.at/2025/04/02/wie-viel-transparenz-und-korruptionsbekaempfung-steckt-im-neuen-regierungsprogramm/), [moment.at](https://www.moment.at/story/informationsfreiheitsgesetz-amtsgeheimnis/)
- **Trust in judiciary**: A Spring 2024 survey found approximately **23%** of Austrians say they distrust the judiciary/legal system. Source: [Statista](https://de.statista.com/statistik/daten/studie/377456/umfrage/umfrage-in-oesterreich-zum-vertrauen-in-justiz-und-rechtssystem/)

## 2. Major Cases

### Case 1 — Ibiza-Affäre (Ibiza-gate), video filmed 2017, published 2019

**Institution:** FPÖ leadership / Vice-Chancellery.

**Mechanism:** A secretly filmed video showed then-Vice-Chancellor and FPÖ leader Heinz-Christian Strache and FPÖ caucus leader Johann Gudenus meeting a woman posing as the niece of a Russian oligarch at a villa in Ibiza, discussing corrupt party-financing schemes, awarding public contracts in exchange for campaign support, and covertly taking control of a nominally independent newspaper. The video showed willingness to negotiate corrupt arrangements but did not itself document a consummated transaction.

**Resignation (documented):** Strache resigned as Vice-Chancellor and FPÖ party leader the day after the video's publication, 18 May 2019; Gudenus also resigned all political offices. This triggered the collapse of the ÖVP–FPÖ coalition government and led to snap elections in September 2019.

**Separately — Strache's own corruption prosecutions, both ended in FINAL ACQUITTALS:**
1. **"Gesetzeskauf" case** (donations-for-legislation): Strache was convicted at first instance in 2021 (15 months conditional) over an alleged link between a private clinic operator's party donation and favorable health-policy legislation. This verdict was **overturned in 2022** by the OLG Wien; a retrial produced a **final acquittal in January 2023**, which is now legally binding. Strache called it an "extremely fair and highly correct procedure."
2. **"Stieglitz"/ASFINAG case** (bribery over a state-company posting): Strache and Gerald Stieglitz were charged with bribery in February 2022; both were **acquitted "in dubio pro reo" in July 2022**, and the OLG Wien **confirmed the acquittal in 2023**, now final.

**Conclusion: Strache has no final corruption conviction as of mid-2026.** His only documented, sourced consequence is the 2019 resignation over the video itself.

**Sources:** [Wikipedia DE](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibiza-Aff%C3%A4re), [ORF](https://orf.at/stories/3165393/), [LTO](https://www.lto.de/recht/nachrichten/n/heinz-christian-strache-freispruch-prozess-korruption-bestechlichkeit-fpoe), [profil timeline](https://www.profil.at/oesterreich/die-ibiza-timeline-was-seit-straches-video-und-dem-ruecktritt-von-sebastian-kurz-geschah/402455439), [euronews](https://de.euronews.com/2023/01/11/korruptionsprozess-freispruch-fuer-ex-fpoe-chef-strache)

### Case 2 — Sebastian Kurz: Falschaussage (False Testimony) Case

**Institution:** Ibiza parliamentary investigative committee testimony concerning appointments to the state holding company ÖBAG.

**Mechanism:** Prosecutors alleged that Kurz, testifying under oath in June 2020, understated his personal influence over the appointment of the ÖBAG supervisory board chairman, contradicted by chat logs and testimony from his former cabinet chief Bernhard Bonelli and ex-ÖBAG chief Thomas Schmid.

**First-instance verdict (26 February 2024, Landesgericht Wien):** Kurz convicted of false testimony, sentenced to 8 months conditional imprisonment; Bernhard Bonelli separately convicted, 6 months conditional.

**Appeal verdict (26 May 2025, Oberlandesgericht Wien) — FINAL:** Kurz's conviction was **overturned — final acquittal.** The appellate panel found he had answered the specific question posed to him correctly and completely, and had not implied his answer was exhaustive on all related points. **Bonelli's conviction (6 months conditional) was upheld and is final** — he is the only person in this case complex with a final conviction. Kurz called the four-year process a "systemic problem" and said the accusations had "collapsed in on themselves."

**Kurz's separate resignation (documented, not a conviction claim):** Kurz resigned as Federal Chancellor on 9 October 2021 amid the broader ÖVP corruption affair (see Case 5), following a WKStA raid connected to Thomas Schmid's chat logs. He resigned as ÖVP party leader shortly after.

**Sources:** [ORF](https://orf.at/stories/3394931/), [LTO](https://www.lto.de/recht/nachrichten/n/oesterreich-sebastian-kurz-freispruch-falschaussage-ibiza-affaere), [OLG Wien Medieninformation](https://www.justiz.gv.at/justiz/medienstelle/pressemitteilungen/medieninformation-des-oberlandesgerichts-wien-zur-causa-kurz.1080.de.html), [profil](https://www.profil.at/oesterreich/freispruch-fuer-kurz-urteil-gegen-seinen-ex-kabinettschef-bestaetigt/403044806)

### Case 3 — BUWOG / Karl-Heinz Grasser Case

**Years:** Conduct 2000–2004 (BUWOG privatization of ~60,000 federal apartments); investigation from 2009; first-instance verdict December 2020; final OGH ruling **25 March 2025** — roughly 15 years of proceedings.

**Institution:** Austrian Ministry of Finance (Grasser was Finance Minister 2000–2007).

**Mechanism:** Grasser and associates allegedly received a kickback of roughly €9.6 million via lobbyist channels in exchange for insider information that helped a bidding consortium win the BUWOG privatization by a margin of about €1 million on a ~€960 million sale — an unusually narrow winning margin suggesting advance knowledge of the competing bid.

**Final OGH ruling, 25 March 2025:** The Austrian Supreme Court confirmed convictions on the core bribery/breach-of-trust charges but overturned the evidence-falsification conviction, and — citing the "exorbitantly long duration" of the roughly 15-year proceedings — substantially reduced sentences:

- **Karl-Heinz Grasser** (former Finance Minister) — **final sentence: 4 years unconditional imprisonment** (reduced from 8 years). He has stated intent to appeal to the European Court of Human Rights, which does not affect the domestic finality of the conviction.
- **Walter Meischberger** (lobbyist, former FPÖ general secretary) — **final sentence: 3.5 years unconditional imprisonment** (reduced from 7 years).
- **Peter Hochegger** (lobbyist/PR consultant) — **final sentence: 3 years, partially conditional imprisonment.**
- **Ernst Karl Plech** (close confidant of Grasser and Meischberger, alleged third recipient of kickback funds) — became **unfit to stand trial** (aged, seriously ill) during proceedings and received no verdict; his case remains procedurally unresolved — he must be named by role only, neither convicted nor acquitted.

**Aftermath:** Grasser filed for private bankruptcy following the ruling. The case is widely cited as Austria's longest-running and highest-profile corruption trial.

**Sources:** [OGH Medieninformation](https://www.ogh.gv.at/medieninformationen/medieninformation-des-ogh-zur-causa-buwog/), [profil](https://www.profil.at/investigativ/rechtskraeftig-vier-jahre-haft-fuer-grasser-wegen-korruption-und-untreue/403026165), [ORF](https://orf.at/stories/3388651/), [PULS24](https://www.puls24.at/news/politik/jetzt-rechtskraeftig-buwog-urteil-das-sind-die-haftstrafen-fuer-grasser-meischberger-co/396788)

### Case 4 — Eurofighter Procurement Scandal

**Years:** Procurement decision 2002–2003; investigations from 2007 to present; **no final Austrian criminal conviction to date.**

**Institution:** Austrian Ministry of Defence / Bundesheer procurement of Eurofighter Typhoon jets from the EADS/Airbus consortium.

**Mechanism:** Allegations of bribery and "black funds" funneled through shell companies (notably "VECTOR Aerospace") and sham contracts to secure the 2003 jet contract and the 2007 partial-cancellation settlement; investigations have implicated roughly 60 named suspects.

**Status: no final Austrian conviction.** The original fraud investigation into the 2003 procurement and 2007 settlement was definitively closed on 11 November 2020 after the OLG Wien rejected complaints against the closure. A **new indictment was filed in 2024** by the WKStA over the sham-contract/VECTOR Aerospace scheme (breach of trust, potential 1–10 year sentences) — **this remains an open, unresolved prosecution, not a conviction.** One senior EADS official was convicted in Germany (final, February 2019) over sham-contract acts — a German, not Austrian, conviction, outside this dossier's scope.

**Alfons Mensdorff-Pouilly** (lobbyist, central figure in the black-fund complex): convicted at first instance in September 2022 of money laundering roughly €2 million (6 months conditional), but the **OLG Wien overturned the conviction into a final acquittal**, finding the legal requirement that he personally received tainted funds from the predicate offense was not met. He has always maintained the money belonged to a deceased relative by marriage. **Excluded from any "convicted" list — final acquittal.**

**Institutional legacy:** Absent final convictions, the Eurofighter affair's documented legacy in Austria is primarily institutional and reputational — parliamentary scrutiny of defense procurement and an ongoing WKStA case — rather than adjudicated corruption.

**Sources:** [Wikipedia DE](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurofighter-Aff%C3%A4re), [WKStA press release](https://www.justiz.gv.at/wksta/wirtschafts-und-korruptionsstaatsanwaltschaft/medienstelle/pressemitteilungen/pressemitteilungen-2024/verfahrenskomplex-eurofighter-anklage-untreue.f63.de.html), [profil](https://www.profil.at/investigativ/mensdorff-freispruch-fuer-mich-war-das-immer-eine-nullnummer/402923377), [derStandard](https://www.derstandard.at/story/3000000227908/eurofighter-freispruch-f252r-mensdorff-pouilly-in-der-berufung)

### Case 5 — ÖVP Corruption Probe / Beinschab-Tool Affair (fake polls for favorable coverage)

**Years:** Conduct c. 2016–2018; scandal broke publicly 6 October 2021 (triggering Kurz's resignation as Chancellor on 9 October 2021); Karmasin trial verdict May 2023; OGH final ruling March 2024.

**Institution:** ÖVP party leadership / Federal Ministry of Finance, in coordination with the tabloid "Österreich."

**Mechanism:** Public funds — including from the Finance Ministry — were allegedly used to pay for favorable newspaper advertisements placed with "Österreich," in exchange for the newspaper commissioning and publishing manipulated opinion polls (conducted by market researcher Sabine Beinschab) intended to boost Kurz's rise to party leadership and the chancellorship. Uncovered via chat logs on a backup drive belonging to former Finance Ministry secretary-general Thomas Schmid.

**Legal status, per person:**
- **Sophie Karmasin** (former Family Minister, ÖVP): convicted May 2023 (first instance) of inciting anti-competitive collusive arrangements, 15 months conditional; acquitted of a separate fraud charge. The **OGH confirmed the conviction in substance but reduced the sentence to 10 months in March 2024 — final/rechtskräftig.**
- **Sabine Beinschab** (market researcher): granted **Kronzeugin (crown witness) status**, her own proceeding discontinued in exchange for testimony against Karmasin. **Not convicted** — name by role only.
- **Thomas Schmid** (former Finance Ministry secretary-general, ex-ÖBAG chief): granted **Kronzeuge status** by the WKStA in November 2024. A separate false-testimony investigation against him was discontinued for lack of initial suspicion. A new complaint over alleged abuse of office was filed in 2025 — status unresolved. **Not convicted** — name by role only.
- **Sebastian Kurz**: named as the alleged principal beneficiary; resigned as Chancellor over this scandal complex. **No final personal conviction of Kurz has been established in the fake-polls complex itself** — treat as under investigation/accusatory only for this specific sub-case, separate from his already-acquitted false-testimony case (Case 2).

**Sources:** [Wikipedia DE — ÖVP-Korruptionsaffäre](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%96VP-Korruptionsaff%C3%A4re), [marktforschung.de](https://www.marktforschung.de/marktforschung/a/gericht-bestaetigt-urteil-gegen-sophie-karmasin-reduziert-jedoch-die-strafe/), [ORF](https://orf.at/stories/3361402/), [kontrast.at](https://kontrast.at/thomas-schmid-oevp/)

### Case 6 — Casinos Austria "Postenschacher" (Patronage) Affair

**Years:** Conduct 2018–2019; WKStA investigation since spring 2019; ongoing as of 2024–2026 with partial closures.

**Institution:** Casinos Austria AG supervisory/executive board; involves FPÖ and ÖVP-linked figures and the gaming company Novomatic.

**Mechanism:** Alleged patronage arrangement in which FPÖ district councillor **Peter Sidlo**, lacking relevant qualifications, was installed as CFO of Casinos Austria in May 2019, allegedly as part of broader political horse-trading connected to gambling-license considerations.

**Status:** Sidlo was removed from his CFO position on 2 December 2019 for "gross breach of duty" — an employment decision, not a court ruling. He subsequently lost a civil lawsuit against Casinos Austria over his dismissal (the OGH ruled the dismissal lawful) — a civil, not criminal, matter. **No final criminal conviction of Sidlo has been established** in available sources; he remains under investigation/accused only. In December 2024, the WKStA discontinued investigations against several other implicated figures, including former Finance Minister Josef Pröll and former bank executives, without conviction — they are named here by role only.

**Sources:** [Wikipedia DE — Casinos-Affäre](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casinos-Aff%C3%A4re), [derStandard](https://www.derstandard.at/story/2000111763116/peter-sidlo-als-casinos-finanzvorstand-abberufen), [kontrast.at](https://kontrast.at/casino-affaere-fpoe-oevp-loeger-sidlo/)

### Case 7 — Telekom Austria Bribery Scandal

**Years:** Conduct 2000–2007; charges filed January 2013; OGH ruling November 2015.

**Institution:** Telekom Austria AG management, in connection with FPÖ/BZÖ politicians and lobbyists during the Wolfgang Schüssel chancellorship (2000–2007).

**Mechanism:** Off-the-books "black cash" slush funds financed illegal party-donation channels to the FPÖ and BZÖ, alongside questionable sponsoring deals, to secure favorable telecom-sector legislation and regulatory treatment.

**Legal status:**
- **Peter Hochegger** (lobbyist, also central to the BUWOG case above): the **OGH confirmed his embezzlement (Untreue) conviction on 25 November 2015 — final.** A separate perjury charge against him was sent back for retrial; that charge's ultimate disposition is not independently confirmed and is not asserted here.
- **Klaus Wittauer**: his verdict became legally binding after the state prosecutor accepted it and Wittauer agreed to his sentence — **final**, though the exact sentence was not confirmed in available sources.
- **Rudolf Fischer** (former Telekom Austria board member): **acquitted, final** — name by role only, excluded from the convicted list.
- **Gernot Schieszler** (former Telekom controlling chief): became a **state's witness (Kronzeuge)** in exchange for immunity — **not convicted**, name by role only.

**Aftermath — Wolfgang Schüssel:** Schüssel served as Chancellor 2000–2007, spanning the Telekom, BUWOG, and Eurofighter scandals; several of his ministers and allies were implicated in multi-million-euro corruption cases. **Schüssel resigned his Nationalrat mandate on 8 September 2011**, a departure his own party and commentators attributed to the cumulative weight of the scandal-heavy era of his chancellorship — documented as a resignation associated with this scandal legacy, though Schüssel himself was not personally charged or convicted.

**Sources:** [Wikipedia DE — Telekom-Affäre](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telekom-Aff%C3%A4re), [Austria-Forum](https://austria-forum.org/af/AustriaWiki/Telekom-Aff%C3%A4re), [oe24](https://www.oe24.at/oesterreich/politik/telekom-affaere-schuessel-warum-er-gehen-muss/39417671)

### Political and institutional aftermath, all cases

Austria's corruption cases share a distinctive pattern rarely seen at this scale elsewhere in Western Europe: a high proportion of first-instance convictions against senior political figures (Strache, Kurz, Mensdorff-Pouilly, and — partially — Grasser's evidence-falsification count) were later reduced or overturned on appeal, and Austria's own GRECO compliance record is among the weakest in the Council of Europe on measures specifically targeting parliamentarians. Commentators and legal analysts have pointed to the "exorbitant" length of proceedings (explicitly cited by the OGH in reducing the BUWOG sentences after 15 years) as both a mitigating factor recognized by courts and a structural weakness that critics argue benefits well-resourced defendants who can sustain lengthy appeals. The establishment of the WKStA (Wirtschafts- und Korruptionsstaatsanwaltschaft, the dedicated Economic and Corruption Prosecutor's Office) in 2011 was itself a direct institutional response to the Telekom Austria and BUWOG scandals, and it remains the central prosecuting authority across nearly every case in this dossier — including the ongoing Eurofighter, Casinos Austria, and ÖVP-era investigations.

### Notable exclusions (per the strict legal rule)

- **Heinz-Christian Strache** — no final corruption conviction; both criminal cases ended in final acquittals. Only the 2019 resignation is reportable as fact.
- **Sebastian Kurz** — final acquittal in the false-testimony case (2025); no established final conviction in the fake-polls complex. Only his 2021 resignation as Chancellor is reportable as fact.
- **Alfons Mensdorff-Pouilly** — final acquittal in the Eurofighter money-laundering case.
- **Ernst Karl Plech** — unresolved due to unfitness to stand trial; neither convicted nor acquitted.
- **Peter Sidlo** — no final criminal conviction established; only a lost civil lawsuit is confirmed.
- **Rudolf Fischer** — final acquittal in the Telekom case.

## 5. Language of Denial

1. **Karl-Heinz Grasser**, immediately after the December 2020 first-instance BUWOG conviction (8 years): *"Ich weiß, dass ich unschuldig bin."* ("I know that I am innocent.") He added he had expected an acquittal and stated: *"Dieses Urteil hat nichts mit Fairness und Gerechtigkeit zu tun... Das Urteil ist unrecht. Massive Verletzung von allem, was Recht ist.
