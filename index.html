02 — How the session runs

Six phases. You already know how to vibe-code, so we spend the time on what code can't tell you.

00 · 20 minBootA real homepage on the wall. What is it trying to make you feel?
01 · 30 minRead the grammarColour, type, furniture, voice. How authority gets rendered in CSS.
02 · 25 minPick a targetOne body, one dossier, one case, one angle.
03 · 45 minClone the skinChrome before content. Pass the civil-servant test.
04 · 45 minPour in the storyTonal deadpan. Every fact sourced. Never wink.
05–06 · 35 minRegister & debrief90 seconds each, in character. Then: where else do you see these tricks?

Convicted or resigned. Nothing else.

Every person named in this kit holds a final criminal conviction for a corruption offence, or resigned over a documented scandal and is described only as having resigned. People on trial, acquitted, or cleared on appeal are left out. We pulled several during research for exactly that reason.

Truth is a defence, and the public record holds up where rumour doesn't. It also makes the work sharper: when you can't exaggerate, you have to be precise, and precision set in a ministry's own typeface does more damage than any insult.
