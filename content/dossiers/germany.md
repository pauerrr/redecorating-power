# GERMANY — Documented Political Corruption Dossier

*Prepared for Domestic Data Streamers workshop. Compiled July 2026. Legal status verified against 2024–2026 sources. Strict rule applied throughout: only FINAL criminal convictions (rechtskräftig — all appeals exhausted or none filed) or documented resignations are treated as established fact. Accused, under-investigation, or acquitted persons are named by role only, never asserted as guilty.*

---

## 1. Country Snapshot

- **Transparency International CPI 2024**: Germany scored **75/100**, ranked **15th of 180** — its lowest-ever score on the index. Source: [Transparency.org CPI 2024](https://www.transparency.org/en/cpi/2024/index/deu)
- **CPI 2025**: Germany scored **77/100**, ranked **10th of 182** — up 2 points and 5 places versus 2024, though still down 4 points over the last decade. Sources: [Transparency.org CPI 2025](https://www.transparency.org/en/cpi/2025/index/deu), [GSK Stockmann](https://gsk.de/en/the-new-transparency-international-corruption-perceptions-index-2025/)
- **Eurobarometer**: EU-wide, 68% of citizens believe corruption is widespread in their country (2024 survey). **In Germany specifically, 57%** consider corruption widespread nationally — for comparison, Greece 98%, Finland 18%. Sources: [Eurobarometer 3217](https://europa.eu/eurobarometer/surveys/detail/3217), [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/my-europe/2025/07/23/do-eu-citizens-believe-that-their-local-and-national-public-institutions-are-corrupt)
- **Cost-of-corruption estimates** (figures vary substantially by methodology — cite as a range, not a single number):
  - Cum-Ex: minimum estimated damage to the German treasury of **≥€10 billion**, concentrated 2001–2011. — [Finanzwende](https://www.finanzwende.de/themen/cumex)
  - Cum-Cum: independent estimate of **€28.5 billion** German-specific damage (2000–2020) versus the German government's own more conservative estimate of **€7.5 billion**. — [Handelsblatt](https://www.handelsblatt.com/politik/deutschland/steuerhinterziehung-bund-schaetzt-cum-cum-schaden-auf-75-milliarden-euro/100143020.html)
  - Combined Cum-Ex/Cum-Cum-style trading, per a 12-country Correctiv investigation: **≥€150 billion** total European damage, of which **~€36 billion** was lost specifically to German tax authorities. — [LTO](https://www.lto.de/recht/kanzleien-unternehmen/k/cum-ex-neue-recherche-steuerbetrug-dividenden-zwoelf-laender-150-milliarden-euro-schaden)
- **GRECO**: The Fifth Round Evaluation found Germany in only **partial/insufficient compliance** with recommendations on preventing corruption in central government and law enforcement; GRECO requested a further progress report by 31 March 2026 (concluded August 2025). Separately, the Fourth Round evaluation (parliamentarians, judges, prosecutors) found Germany had one of the **highest non-implementation rates for recommendations concerning MPs: 16.6%** (versus 10.9% for judges, 8.4% for prosecutors). Sources: [Council of Europe](https://www.coe.int/en/web/greco/-/greco-publishes-report-on-germany-s-progress-in-anti-corruption-measures-regarding-the-central-government-and-law-enforcement), [eucrim](https://eucrim.eu/news/greco-fourth-round-evaluation-report-germany/)
- **Official crime statistics (BKA Bundeslagebild Korruption)**: **3,841** registered corruption offenses in 2023 (+6.7% YoY); **2,926** in 2024 (−23.8% YoY), with recorded financial damage falling to **€36 million** (−36.8% YoY). Sources: [BKA 2023](https://www.bka.de/SharedDocs/Kurzmeldungen/DE/Kurzmeldungen/240910_BLB_Korruption2023.html), [BKA 2024](https://www.bka.de/SharedDocs/Kurzmeldungen/DE/Kurzmeldungen/251028_BLB_Korruption2024.html)

## 2. Major Cases

### Case 1 — CDU Illegal Party Donations Scandal (CDU-Spendenaffäre), 1993–2000

**Institution:** CDU federal party, under the chairmanship of Helmut Kohl.

**Mechanism:** Off-the-books "black accounts" held undeclared cash donations — including from arms dealer Karlheinz Schreiber — via illegal foreign bank accounts in Switzerland and Liechtenstein, concealing more than 30 million Deutschmarks, in violation of Germany's Party Finance Act.

**Legal status — precise:**
- **Helmut Kohl (former Chancellor, CDU Chairman): was NOT criminally convicted.** The investigation for breach of trust (Untreue) against him was **discontinued by the Bonn Regional Court (Landgericht Bonn) in February 2001 under §153a StPO**, against payment of a **DM 300,000 money penalty (Geldauflage)**, citing minor culpability. This is a procedural settlement, not a verdict of guilt, and must never be described as a "conviction." Kohl maintained he had given his "word of honor" (Ehrenwort) not to name four or five donors — a justification later disputed by fellow CDU figure Wolfgang Schäuble. Sources: [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/politics/2001/02/09/settlement-reported-in-kohl-scandal/55532d57-4b3e-4109-95d6-e89f4f4ab633/), [CNN](http://edition.cnn.com/2001/WORLD/europe/06/08/germany.kohl/index.html), [Tagesspiegel](https://www.tagesspiegel.de/gesellschaft/medien/helmut-kohls-ehrenwort-war-ein-ablenkungsmanover-5675329.html)
- **Walther Leisler Kiep** (former CDU Federal Treasurer): **Convicted** of tax evasion by the Landgericht Augsburg in 2001, for failing to declare roughly 16,000 DM in interest income on his 1991 tax return. Fined **DM 45,000**. Sources: [Tagesspiegel](https://www.tagesspiegel.de/politik/cdu-spendenaffare-hohe-geldstrafe-fur-kiep-758299.html), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walther_Leisler_Kiep)

**Aftermath:** A Bundestag investigative committee was established in December 1999; the scandal triggered reform of German party-finance law and lasting reputational damage to the CDU, even though its chairman was never criminally convicted.

**Sources:** [CDU-Spendenaffäre, Wikipedia DE](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/CDU-Spendenaff%C3%A4re), [Bundestag archive](https://www.bundestag.de/dokumente/textarchiv/2024/kw48-kalenderblatt-parteispenden-1030718)

### Case 2 — Cum-Ex Trading Scandal, c. 2001–2012 (prosecutions continuing into 2026)

**Institution:** Private banks, law firms, and investors exploiting a dividend-tax loophole.

**Mechanism:** Coordinated share trades around dividend record dates among multiple parties generated duplicate tax-refund certificates, allowing a tax paid once to be reclaimed multiple times.

**Legal status — Hanno Berger (lawyer, designer of the scheme): FINAL, doubly convicted.**
- **Landgericht Bonn, December 2022**: convicted of especially serious tax evasion in three cases, sentenced to **8 years**, plus repayment of **€13.6 million**. The Bundesgerichtshof (BGH) rejected his appeal in **September 2023**, making this conviction final.
- **Landgericht Wiesbaden, 30 May 2023**: separately convicted of especially serious tax evasion in three further cases (trades from 2006–2008 involving the late Berlin entrepreneur Rafael Roth, with €113 million improperly reclaimed), sentenced to **8 years 3 months**. The BGH rejected this appeal on **29 October 2024**, making it final; a further BGH ruling on 8 July 2025 confirmed confiscation of €1.1 million in criminal proceeds.
- **May 2026**: the Landgericht Bonn consolidated both final sentences into a combined total sentence (Gesamtstrafe) of **10 years**.
- Berger was extradited from Switzerland to Germany in 2022 to face these charges and continues to deny wrongdoing.

**Scale:** German investigators have, per reporting, pursued roughly **1,500 suspects** tied to Cum-Ex overall; most individual cases beyond Berger's remain unresolved or in progress and are not named here as convictions.

**Sources:** [LTO — Bonn verdict](https://www.lto.de/recht/nachrichten/n/hanno-berger-prozess-landgericht-bonn-urteil-steuerhinterziehung-betrug-verkuendung-acht-jahre-haft-az-62kls220-cum-ex), [LTO — BGH confirms Bonn](https://www.lto.de/recht/nachrichten/n/haftstrafe-fuer-hanno-berger-bgh-bestaetigt-das-lg-bonn), [LTO — BGH confirms Wiesbaden](https://www.lto.de/recht/nachrichten/n/bgh-1-str-58-24-hanno-berger-cum-ex-revision-verworfen-lg-wiesbaden), [Hessenschau](https://www.hessenschau.de/wirtschaft/bgh-bestaetigt-urteil-cum-ex-anwalt-berger-droht-deutlich-hoehere-strafe-v1,hanno-berger-revision-100.html), [beck-aktuell — consolidated sentence](https://www.beck-aktuell.de/heute-im-recht/rechtsprechung/cum-ex-hanno-berger-gesamtstrafe-zehn-jahre-haft-2026-05-13)

### Case 3 — Maskenaffäre (COVID-19 mask procurement scandal), 2020–2021

**Institution:** CDU/CSU members of the Bundestag and Bavarian Landtag acting as paid intermediaries in pandemic-era PPE procurement.

**Legal status — precise, per person:**
- **Georg Nüsslein** (CSU, then-Bundestag member) and **Alfred Sauter** (CSU, Bavarian state MP, former Bavarian Justice Minister): both brokered mask-supply deals for commissions (Nüsslein reportedly earned €660,000 on a roughly €35 million deal). **Neither was criminally convicted.** The Bundesgerichtshof ruled in **July 2022** that brokering the deals for commission did not legally constitute punishable bribery of a mandate-holder under German law, because the favor did not concern the "exercise of the mandate" in the narrow legal sense. Nüsslein's seized assets (€3.6 million) were ordered released to him. Both must be named only by role/accusation, never as "convicted." Sources: [Berliner Zeitung](https://www.berliner-zeitung.de/news/urteil-csu-abgeordnete-georg-nuesslein-und-alfred-sauter-haben-sich-in-maskenaffaere-nicht-strafbar-gemacht-li.246043), [Transparency Deutschland](https://www.transparency.de/aktuelles/detail/article/maskenaffaere-bgh-bestaetigt-straffreiheit-von-nuesslein-und-sauter)
- **Nikolas Löbel** (CDU, Bundestag member, Mannheim): brokered a separate PPE delivery contract and received a **€250,000** commission. He **resigned** his Bundestag mandate and district chairmanship in March 2021, publicly apologizing: *"Dafür möchte ich mich bei allen Bürgerinnen und Bürgern dieses Landes entschuldigen."* Prosecutors in Stuttgart announced at the end of March 2021 that they would **not open a formal investigation** for lack of initial suspicion — so there was no criminal case at all, only the resignation. This is a clean, sourced example of "resignation over documented scandal." Sources: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikolas_L%C3%B6bel), [t-online](https://www.t-online.de/nachrichten/deutschland/innenpolitik/id_89612808/maskenaffaere-nikolas-loebel-legt-bundestagsmandat-nieder-und-verlaesst-cdu.html)
- **Andrea Tandler** (businesswoman, daughter of former CSU official Gerold Tandler — a private individual, not herself a politician): earned an estimated **€48 million** in commissions from mask deals. **Convicted** of tax evasion by the Landgericht München I in December 2023, originally sentenced to 4 years 5 months. On appeal, the **Bundesgerichtshof reduced the sentence on 20 April 2025** (case 1 StR 238/24), partially discontinuing one charge for insufficient evidence, to **3 years — now final/rechtskräftig**. Her partner was correspondingly convicted with a final reduced sentence of 3 years. Sources: [LTO — original verdict](https://www.lto.de/recht/nachrichten/n/urteil-lg-muenchen-i-maskenaffaere-andrea-tandler-corona-steuerhinterziehung), [LTO — BGH reduction](https://www.lto.de/recht/nachrichten/n/1str23824-bgh-teilweise-verfahrenseinstellung-revision-geringere-strafe-tandler), [Tagesspiegel](https://www.tagesspiegel.de/politik/bayerische-maskenaffare-gericht-reduziert-haftstrafe-fur-politikertochter-tandler-14011244.html)

**Aftermath:** A Bavarian Landtag investigative committee ("Untersuchungsausschuss Maske") ran from December 2021 to May 2023 and exposed a legal gap — facilitation-for-profit outside formal parliamentary duties was not criminally punishable — prompting Transparency Deutschland to call for criminal-law reform.

**Sources:** [abgeordnetenwatch](https://www.abgeordnetenwatch.de/kampagnen/maskendeals-waren-legal-eine-reform-des-strafrechts-ist-dringend-geboten), [Bayerischer Landtag](https://www.bayern.landtag.de/parlament/gremien/untersuchungsausschuesse/untersuchungsausschuss-maske/)

### Case 4 — Wirecard Collapse, 2015–2020 (criminal trial not yet concluded as of mid-2026)

**Institution:** Wirecard AG, and the supervisory failures of financial regulator BaFin and auditor EY.

**Mechanism:** Fabrication of approximately **€1.9 billion** in fictitious cash balances (supposedly held in Philippine trustee accounts) and fraudulent Asian revenue, inflating the balance sheet until the company's June 2020 collapse — Germany's largest post-war corporate fraud.

**Legal status — precise: no individual final criminal conviction can be reported.**
- **Markus Braun** (former CEO): his criminal trial at the Landgericht München I (begun December 2022, the longest economic-crime trial in German history) **remains ongoing and unconcluded** as of this dossier's compilation in mid-2026, with a verdict anticipated in 2026 but no confirmed date. Prosecutors have signaled they will seek a sentence of "more than ten years" if he is convicted. **Braun must not be described as convicted — he denies all charges and blames the fugitive former COO and a confessed co-defendant.** A **separate civil judgment** (not a criminal conviction) ordered Braun and other former executives to pay €140 million in civil damages — this is a distinct legal track and must not be conflated with a criminal conviction.
- **Oliver Bellenhaus** (former Wirecard Dubai representative, confessed and cooperating): released from over three years of pre-trial detention under strict conditions; has confessed to fabricating contracts, but **no final verdict/sentence for him was confirmed** as of mid-2026 — his case remains part of the same unconcluded Munich trial track.
- **Jan Marsalek** (former COO): fled Germany on 18 June 2020 and remains a **fugitive** with an active international arrest warrant, reportedly living in Moscow under a Russian identity and alleged by investigators to be a Russian intelligence asset since around 2010. He has not been tried.

**Institutional findings (no personal guilt asserted):** The Bundestag's Third Committee of Inquiry (19th electoral term, active November 2020 – June 2021; 32 sessions, 110 witnesses) concluded that the auditor EY and the regulator BaFin both failed professionally — BaFin acted too slowly, initially treating Wirecard as a "victim of speculators" and imposing a short-selling ban on its shares rather than investigating the company; EY focused disproportionately on the regulated banking subsidiary and gave insufficient scrutiny to Wirecard AG itself, where the fraud occurred. The 2021 "Act to Strengthen Financial Market Integrity" tightened balance-sheet controls, audit rules, and BaFin's investigative powers as a direct legislative consequence.

**Sources:** [LTO](https://www.lto.de/recht/kanzleien-unternehmen/k/gericht-skizziert-zeitplan-fuer-strafprozess-wirecard-muenchen-4kls402js10819422), [Handelsblatt](https://www.handelsblatt.com/finanzen/banken-versicherungen/wirecard-markus-braun-muss-mit-haftstrafe-von-mehr-als-zehn-jahren-rechnen/100189925.html), [LTO on Bellenhaus release](https://www.lto.de/recht/kanzleien-unternehmen/k/strafprozess-entlassung-u-haft-oliver-bellenhaus-4kls402js10819422-jva-stadelheim-muenchen-markus-braun-insolvenz-wirecard), [Wikipedia — Marsalek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jan_Marsalek), [Bundestag final report](https://www.bundestag.de/dokumente/textarchiv/2021/kw25-de-3ua-bericht-847030), [bpb.de](https://www.bpb.de/kurz-knapp/hintergrund-aktuell/562462/der-wirecard-skandal-und-die-bafin/)

### Case 5 — Philipp Amthor / Augustus Intelligence Lobbying Affair, 2020 (reputational, non-criminal)

**Precise account:** CDU Bundestag member Philipp Amthor lobbied the Federal Economics Ministry on behalf of the U.S. AI start-up Augustus Intelligence, arranging ministry meetings for the company. Months later he received 2,817 stock options and a director position from the company. He publicly denied wrongdoing at the time ("Ich bin nicht käuflich" — "I am not for sale"). In August 2020 he ended the arrangement and returned the stock options and directorship. **He did not resign his Bundestag seat**; he did withdraw his candidacy for CDU state chairman in Mecklenburg-Vorpommern. The episode is properly characterized as a reputational lobbying scandal with a partial rollback of outside roles — not a resignation from elected office, and not a criminal case.

**Sources:** [abgeordnetenwatch](https://www.abgeordnetenwatch.de/recherchen/lobbyismus/wie-philipp-amthor-zum-tueroeffner-fuer-augustus-intelligence-wurde), [Tagesspiegel](https://www.tagesspiegel.de/politik/amthor-lasst-nebenbeschaftigung-fur-wirtschaftskanzlei-ruhen-4176541.html)

### Case 6 — Christian Wulff / "Kreditaffäre" (resignation, later acquitted)

**Precise account:** In December 2011, allegations surfaced that Christian Wulff, then Federal President (previously Minister-President of Lower Saxony), had inaccurately answered a state-parliament inquiry about his home financing, concealing a favorable private loan, and separately faced allegations of accepting hospitality from a film producer. On 16 February 2012, Hanover prosecutors announced sufficient indications of an initial suspicion of accepting advantages (Vorteilsannahme) and requested the lifting of his presidential immunity. **Wulff resigned as Federal President on 17 February 2012**, citing the loss of trust required for the office — a legitimate, well-documented example of resignation over scandal, occurring before any trial. In **February 2014, the Landgericht Hannover acquitted Wulff of the charge**, a final/rechtskräftig acquittal. **He must therefore be excluded from any "convicted" list** — only the resignation itself is reportable as fact.

**Sources:** [Wulff-Affäre, Wikipedia DE](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wulff-Aff%C3%A4re), [LTO](https://www.lto.de/recht/hintergruende/h/lg-hannover-urteil-40kls612-wulff-korruption-freispruch), [Bundespräsident.de — resignation speech](https://www.bundespraesident.de/SharedDocs/Reden/DE/Christian-Wulff/Reden/2012/02/120217-Erklaerung.html)

### Case 7 — Bundeswehr "Berateraffäre" (external consultants scandal), 2018–2020 (institutional, not a corruption conviction)

**Institution:** Federal Ministry of Defence, under then-Defence Minister Ursula von der Leyen.

**Mechanism:** External IT and management consultants were awarded contracts worth a low three-digit-million-euro sum for defense modernization projects, with the Bundestag's investigative committee documenting numerous procedural and legal violations in how those contracts were awarded (including inadequate documentation, potential circumvention of public-procurement rules, and close personal ties between ministry staff and consulting firms).

**Legal status:** The Bundestag investigative committee's final report made **no legal or direct political accusation of personal wrongdoing against von der Leyen**. Berlin prosecutors separately requested Bundestag files to assess whether to open criminal investigations, including into deleted data on her official mobile phones, but **no criminal charges or convictions against her or named subordinates were confirmed**. This case is presented here as an institutional mismanagement and oversight scandal — illustrating systemic procurement weaknesses — not as a corruption conviction. Von der Leyen left the Defence Ministry in 2019 to become European Commission President, a transition unrelated in timing to any resignation-over-scandal framing, and she must not be listed as convicted or as having resigned over this affair.

**Aftermath:** The episode prompted tightened Bundestag oversight of ministry procurement processes and stricter documentation requirements for external consulting contracts across federal ministries.

**Sources:** [bundeswehr-journal](https://www.bundeswehr-journal.de/2020/abschlussbericht-berateraffaere-von-der-leyen-entlastet/), [t-online](https://www.t-online.de/nachrichten/deutschland/innenpolitik/id_87555930/ursula-von-der-leyen-berateraffaere-im-verteidigungsministerium-staatsanwalt-fordert-akten-an.html)
